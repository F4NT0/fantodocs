# What is an LLM?

LLM stands for <span style="color: orange">L</span>arge <span style="color: orange">L</span>anguage <span style="color: orange">M</span>odels

LLMs are experts at recognizing complex patterns!

They identify correlations, mimic structures, and predict what is likely to be requested next.

At their core, LLMs generate recognized patterns as responses and do not reason logically about the subject, which is why Prompt Engineering matters!
